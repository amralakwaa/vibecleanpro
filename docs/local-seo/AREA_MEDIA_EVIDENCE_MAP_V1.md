## What "Evidence" Means

### Minimum for publication (1 project)
- 2+ real photos from an actual job at that area (not stock, not AI)
- Photos show: before state OR during work OR after result (or any combination)
- Project record in DB with: service type, area FK, owner_confirmed_at timestamp
- No client name required — location (neighborhood only) is sufficient

### Strong evidence (3+ projects)
- Multiple projects showing different service types (e.g., post-construction + villa deep clean)
- Before/after pairs preferred
- Variety of scopes (apartment, villa, commercial)

### Gold standard (5+ projects with before/after pairs)
- Enables the "شواهد العمل" (evidence band) section to be fully populated
- Best competitive differentiation

## Media Sourcing Rules

### Allowed
- Photos taken by the team during or after a real job
- Photos shared by the client (with permission)
- Drone shots of area with our vehicles visible (rare but allowed)

### Prohibited
- Stock photos from any stock library
- AI-generated images
- Photos from other companies' portfolios
- Photos from a different city or country
- Photos showing a different area than labeled
- Before photos fabricated to make the after look better

## Owner Action Items

To unlock the first area page (التعاون recommended), the owner needs to:

1. **Find** 1 recent completed job in التعاون (or العارض or القيروان)
2. **Photograph**: minimum 2 shots — one during/before, one after
3. **Document**: which service was performed, rough scope (apartment size, office floor, etc.)
4. **Upload** via admin panel: Projects → New → assign to area → attach photos
5. **Set `owner_confirmed_at`** on the project record (confirms it's real work)

Once this is done, run the publishing gate checklist and the area is ready to go live.
